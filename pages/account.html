sign in

Enter your email and we'll send you a link. There is no password to remember.

your data

Download a copy of everything in your diary (photos included) at any time, and restore it here if you ever need to. Restoring replaces what is in this browser with the backup.

privacy

Your entries stay on this device unless you sign in. When signed in, they are stored privately under your account, and only you can read them. Voice notes are sent to Deepgram to be turned into text; the audio is not kept by this app. When signed in, product photos are stored as private files under your account too.

delete my account

This permanently deletes your account and everything saved online. Data on this device is kept unless you clear it.
